# Requirements: skeleton core

## Introduction
The reusable interviewer engine in `skeleton/`. It takes a schema and runs an interview until the person approves a complete, valid record. It has no UI; apps provide that.

## Requirements

### 1. Schema
**User story:** As an app builder, I describe what my interviewer must learn in one schema file, so that a new app needs no engine code.
1. WHEN an app loads a schema THEN the skeleton SHALL accept JSON Schema 2020-12 with an optional `x-interview` object per field (question hint, examples, language, sensitive flag, ask order).
2. IF a schema is invalid or uses an unknown `x-interview` key THEN loading SHALL fail with a message naming the field.
3. The skeleton SHALL support nested objects and arrays of objects (e.g. several tours, several ingredients).

### 2. Interview state
**User story:** As a person being interviewed, I can answer, skip, go back and change answers, so that I stay in control.
1. Every field SHALL be in exactly one state: `empty`, `proposed`, `confirmed`, `skipped`, or `conflict`.
2. WHEN the person says to skip THEN the field SHALL become `skipped` and not be asked again unless the person reopens it.
3. WHEN the person changes an earlier answer THEN the field SHALL update and any summary approval SHALL be invalidated.
4. A session SHALL serialize to JSON and resume to an identical state.

### 3. Asking
**User story:** As a person being interviewed, I get a few clear questions at a time in my language.
1. The planner SHALL ask at most 3 questions per turn, required fields first, respecting `ask order`.
2. Questions SHALL be in the session language.
3. WHEN all required fields are `confirmed` or `skipped` THEN the interview SHALL move to the approval step.

### 4. Understanding replies
**User story:** As a person, I can answer naturally, in any order, and the bot picks out what I meant.
1. WHEN the person replies THEN the extractor SHALL propose values for any fields the reply answers, including fields not asked this turn.
2. Proposed values SHALL be validated against the schema before they are stored; invalid values SHALL become a follow-up question, not a stored value.
3. IF a reply contradicts a confirmed value THEN the field SHALL become `conflict` and the next turn SHALL ask the person which is right.
4. The extractor SHALL never fill a field from model knowledge; every stored value SHALL keep the reply text (or source) it came from.
5. IF a reply is vague (e.g. "early morning") THEN the bot SHALL propose a concrete value and ask the person to confirm it.

### 5. Approval and export
**User story:** As a person, I see exactly what will be saved and approve it.
1. The approval summary SHALL be built by code from stored values, never generated by the model.
2. The summary SHALL carry a content hash; approval SHALL record that hash, and export SHALL refuse if the record changed after approval.
3. Exporters SHALL produce JSON (valid against the schema) and Markdown.

### 6. Providers and tests
1. All model calls SHALL go through a `ModelProvider` interface with a Bedrock implementation and a scripted fake.
2. The whole interview SHALL be testable end to end with the fake provider and no network.
3. Property-based tests SHALL cover: session serialize/resume round trip, state transitions never leaving a field in two states, and summary hash changing whenever any confirmed value changes.
